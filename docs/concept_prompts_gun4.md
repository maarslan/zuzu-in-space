# Gün 4 – Gemi Parçaları ve Hangar Komutları

Araç: ChatGPT (hakkı biterse Gemini). Görseller `docs/concepts/` klasörüne.

## 1. Gemi parçaları sayfası
Yeni sohbet, `gemi_a2.png` yüklenir. Dosya: `parcalar.png`
```
Using the attached spaceship as the exact design reference (turquoise and green clay flying saucer of a family of little green aliens, with leg jets and a glowing capsule energy tank), create a clean parts sheet: eight separate spaceship parts arranged in a neat grid of 4 columns and 2 rows, each part floating alone with space around it, all in side view, same lighting.
Top row, left to right:
1. LIGHT SHELL: the saucer shell made thin and lightweight, pale turquoise, a few small round holes like a colander, looks light and a bit rattly.
2. STURDY SHELL: the saucer shell made thick and heavy, darker turquoise, a wide chunky rim and rounded armor plates, looks strong and heavy.
3. SMALL LEG JETS: a set of three short chunky legs with small round jet nozzles in the feet, small soft yellow flames.
4. BIG LEG JETS: a set of three thicker legs with big double jet nozzles in the feet, big bright yellow flames.
Bottom row, left to right:
5. SMALL ENERGY CAPSULE: a small round glass capsule full of glowing yellow liquid with a green clay cap.
6. BIG ENERGY CAPSULE: a big double glass capsule full of glowing yellow liquid, clearly bigger and heavier.
7. STABILIZER: a pair of small rounded fins in green with a little spinning-top symbol, to keep the ship balanced.
8. SOFT LANDING PADS: three soft bouncy cushion pads like marshmallows in cream and turquoise, that fit under the feet.
Plain light grey background, soft studio lighting, no ground shadow, no depth of field. Style: clay-like 3D render, as if handmade from soft modeling clay: rounded chunky forms, subtle fingerprint texture, matte surfaces, warm low-angle sunlight with soft shadows, gentle rim light, cozy handcrafted feel, child-friendly. No text, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no humans.
```

## 2. Hangar ekranı taslağı
Yeni sohbet, `gemi_a2.png` ve `robot_final.png` yüklenir. Dosya: `hangar_taslak.png`
```
A wide 16:9 mobile game screen mockup for children aged 5-10: the cozy spaceship hangar of a family of little green aliens. Use the first attached image for the spaceship and the second for the helper robot, keep their exact designs.
Layout:
- Center: the turquoise saucer spaceship from the first image, big, in side view, parked on a round launch pad, legs folded.
- Bottom: a horizontal strip of rounded clay part cards, each card shows one spaceship part (shells, leg jets, energy capsules, stabilizer, landing pads); one card is being dragged up toward the ship by a cartoon finger icon.
- Left side: four vertical rounded progress bars, each with an icon on top: a feather-to-anvil icon (weight), a flame icon (thrust), a glowing capsule icon (energy), a spinning-top icon (balance). The thrust bar has a small dashed line showing the needed level. No numbers.
- Bottom right: a big round green launch button with a rocket-flame symbol.
- Top right: a big round pause button.
- The teapot helper robot from the second image stands next to the ship pointing at the thrust bar, dropping a bolt.
- Background: warm, calm hangar interior with soft round shapes, darker and less saturated than the ship so the ship and buttons stand out. UI panels in deep navy with yellow and cyan highlights.
Readable on a small phone screen, big touch targets, no clutter. Style: clay-like 3D render, as if handmade from soft modeling clay: rounded chunky forms, subtle fingerprint texture, matte surfaces, warm low-angle light, gentle rim light, cozy handcrafted feel, child-friendly. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no humans.
```
